General guide only — always weigh against what actually changed: ≤15% increase is typically normal for routine updates · 15–40% warrants a quick look at what was added · >40% is worth confirming with the developer before sign-off.

Note on AAB/IPA: an .aab's raw size is a proxy, not the exact size users download — Play Store generates optimized, device-specific split APKs from it. Likewise, an .ipa's raw size can differ from the App Store's on-device download size once Apple applies thinning/slicing for each device. For the most accurate download-size comparison, cross-check against Play Console / App Store Connect's estimated download size for both builds.
